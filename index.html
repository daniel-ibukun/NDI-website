WHO WE ARE

Raising girls who know their worth.

NDI exists to give girls practical tools, safe spaces and positive guidance at the moments that can shape the rest of their lives.

Through school outreach, workshops and mentorship, we help girls build self-esteem, protect their dignity, handle pressure wisely, pursue education and discover their purpose.

“A girl who knows her worth, protects her future.”
